# Kolors

The entry page for Kolors. Every system in [Palettes](#palettes) is drawn from the library in [Colours](#colours).

## Structure of a system
- **Two brand colours** from `/colors`: a primary (buttons, links, selected states) and an accent (one highlight per view, badges, the focus ring). They sit at least 35° apart in hue, so focus and highlights never blend into primary controls.
- **Two logo-only colours** from `/colors`: a spark (the signal dot or highlight) and an ink (wordmark, light-theme text, dark-theme page background). Never used as UI colours.
- **Generated scales:** each brand anchor gets a 50–950 scale in OKLCH, following Harbour Signal's lightness curve and per-step chroma. The anchor keeps its exact value at its nearest step.
- **Plain greys:** paper `#FAFAFA`, white surfaces, and a neutral ramp tinted only imperceptibly by the ink's hue.

## Light and dark
- Light: paper page, white surfaces and raised surfaces, a sunken grey for wells. Text is the ink.
- Dark: the ink is the page. Surfaces, raised surfaces and borders step lighter from it; text is paper.
- The primary and accent use a deeper step in light mode (white labels at 4.5:1 or more) and a lighter step in dark mode (ink labels at 7:1 or more).

## Status, charts and checks
- Success, warning and danger start from Harbour Signal's values and are re-hued within their family when a brand colour sits too close. Status always carries an icon and a word.
- Charts use five accent shades plus the primary as the one contrasting series, each at least 3:1 on the card.
- Every system passes a contrast audit in both themes: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for control borders, focus rings, fills and chart marks.

## Rebuilding
`python3 -I tools/build_palettes.py` regenerates every system from `tools/palette_specs.json`, then the Palettes and Colours sections below. `python3 -I tools/build_indexes.py` refreshes only those two sections.
